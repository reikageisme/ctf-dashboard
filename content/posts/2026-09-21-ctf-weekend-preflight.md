# CTF weekend preflight

Good preparation does not solve challenges for us, but it gives every solver more uninterrupted time to think.

## Before the clock starts

- Confirm the event timezone, start time and team-size restriction.
- Assign a first-pass owner for web, pwn, reverse, crypto and forensics.
- Test the shared notes, file exchange and voice channel.
- Prepare clean workspaces and snapshots for risky binaries or attachments.
- Agree on a short naming convention for challenges and evidence.

## During the event

Keep notes even when an approach fails. A two-line record of a dead end prevents three teammates from repeating it. When a challenge changes hands, leave a clear state: what is known, what was tried and what should happen next.

## After the event

Archive only the material that can be published under the event rules. Turn the strongest solutions into writeups while the reasoning is still fresh, then link them back to the corresponding mission in the dashboard.
