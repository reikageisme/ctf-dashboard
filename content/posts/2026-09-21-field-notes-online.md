# Field notes are online

This dashboard now has a permanent place for writeups and short technical notes. We built it to keep useful details close to the team record instead of scattering them across chat history.

## What belongs here

- Post-event writeups with the path from first clue to final flag.
- Small tools, scripts and debugging techniques that helped during an operation.
- Pre-event notes that make the next CTF less chaotic.

The format stays deliberately simple: one Markdown file per post, stored in `content/posts`. That keeps the archive portable, reviewable and easy to publish with the rest of the site.

## Next transmission

The next real challenge writeup will appear here after an event allows public solutions. Until then, the archive remains ready.
